About me

I’m James Smith. I got into media production through sound design, and live production made me just as interested in what happens behind the scenes.

I lead AV work for a local church, where I help mix services, troubleshoot the livestream, and document the setups volunteers use. I also run Signal Path, my independent AV practice.

Outside the booth

I write fiction, play games, hike, and like figuring out how things are made. Alaina and I are building PuureHof around gardening, cooking, and hands-on projects.

How I work

I like projects where creative decisions and technical details matter equally. Whether I’m mixing, testing a stream, or writing a guide, I want the result to make sense to the next person who uses it.
