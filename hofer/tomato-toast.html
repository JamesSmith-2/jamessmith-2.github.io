SAMPLE RECIPE · 15 MINUTES · SERVES 2

Garden tomato & herb toast

Höfer House · Concept preview

A simple lunch for the days when the tomatoes do most of the work.

Gather

  • 2 thick slices of sourdough
  • 2 ripe tomatoes, sliced
  • ½ cup ricotta
  • 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • A handful of fresh basil
  • Salt and freshly ground pepper

Make it

  1. Toast the bread until golden and crisp.
  2. Spread each slice with ricotta. Layer on the tomatoes.
  3. Drizzle with olive oil, scatter with torn basil, and season to taste.

Make it your own: add lemon zest to the ricotta or finish with a splash of balsamic vinegar.
