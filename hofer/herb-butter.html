SAMPLE RECIPE · 10 MINUTES · MAKES ABOUT ½ CUP

Lemon & garden herb butter

Höfer House · Concept preview

A small kitchen pleasure for warm bread, roasted vegetables, or corn on the cob.

Gather

  • ½ cup softened unsalted butter
  • 2 tablespoons finely chopped parsley
  • 1 tablespoon finely chopped chives
  • 1 teaspoon finely grated lemon zest
  • A pinch of salt, to taste

Make it

  1. Wash and thoroughly dry the herbs, then chop finely.
  2. Mash the butter, herbs, lemon zest, and salt together with a fork.
  3. Spoon into a small covered dish and refrigerate until serving.

Keep refrigerated and use within four days, or freeze in small portions for later.
