SAMPLE GROWING GUIDE · 8 MIN READ

Your first kitchen garden

Höfer House · Concept preview

Start with the things you love to eat. A small, well-tended patch can bring more joy than a large garden that feels like another chore.

Find your sunny spot

Watch the light over a full day. Most fruiting vegetables and culinary herbs do best with at least six hours of direct sunlight. Choose a spot close to water and easy to visit.

Keep the first planting simple

  • Try a pot of basil, a container of leaf lettuce, and one tomato plant.
  • Use containers with drainage holes and a potting mix made for containers.
  • Check the plant label for mature size and spacing before planting.

Make a small daily ritual

Feel the soil before watering, look underneath the leaves, and harvest a little when plants are ready. Adjust planting dates to your local last frost and the needs of each variety.
