SAMPLE HERB GUIDE

Basil, from pot to pesto

Höfer Haus · Concept preview

Sweet basil brings a little summer to the windowsill, patio, or kitchen garden.

Light & warmth

Give basil a sunny, warm spot. Move it outside after frost danger has passed and nights are reliably warm. Protect it from cold snaps.

Water & drainage

Check the top inch of potting mix. Water when it starts to feel dry, letting excess drain away. Avoid leaving the pot standing in water.

Harvest for a fuller plant

Once established, snip stems just above a pair of leaves to encourage branching. Leave plenty of foliage, and pinch off flower buds if you want to keep harvesting tender leaves.

Kitchen idea: tear a few leaves over tomato toast just before serving.
