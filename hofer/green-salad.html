SAMPLE RECIPE · 20 MINUTES · SERVES 4

The just-picked green salad

Hofer House · Concept preview

A flexible bowl for whatever is fresh, crisp, and ready to gather.

Gather

  • 6 cups washed and dried mixed salad greens
  • 1 cucumber, thinly sliced
  • 4 radishes, thinly sliced
  • A small handful of parsley or dill
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Make it

  1. Whisk the olive oil, lemon juice, mustard, salt, and pepper in a small bowl.
  2. Place the greens, cucumber, radishes, and herbs in a serving bowl.
  3. Add a little dressing, toss gently, and taste. Add more dressing as needed and serve right away.

Illustrative recipe; photography is for mood and may differ from the finished dish.
