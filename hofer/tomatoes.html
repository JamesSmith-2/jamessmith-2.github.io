SAMPLE PLANT GUIDE

A good start for tomatoes

Hofer House · Concept preview

Choose a variety that fits your space and growing season. A compact patio tomato is a friendly first choice for a large container.

Give roots room

Choose a substantial pot with drainage; many compact varieties do well in a container of at least five gallons, while larger varieties need more. Follow your variety’s recommendations.

Sun, support, consistency

  • Choose a location with six to eight hours of direct sun.
  • Add a cage or stake at planting time.
  • Water the soil deeply when needed and keep moisture reasonably consistent.
  • Use a vegetable fertilizer according to its label.

Let the season guide you

Plant outdoors after frost danger has passed and the soil has warmed. Pick ripe fruit regularly and check leaves for early signs of trouble.
